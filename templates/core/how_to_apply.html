How to apply for an NHF card

Apply in person and get your card in about {{ p.same_day_minutes }} minutes, send the form by mail, or let your doctor apply for you online. The card never expires.

Questions? Call toll free {{ p.toll_free_numbers }}

{# ---------- The walk-through ---------- #}

Step by step

Choose how you will apply. Each step shows who does it, what happens, and what NHF staff see on their screen.

{# ===== In person ===== #}
  1. Step 1 · Who
    You

    Get an application form

    Pick up the NHF Card / Change Request form at any card centre or your doctor's office, or download it from nhf.org.jm.

    Have your TRN ready. Children need their own TRN, not a parent's.

    What NHF staff see
    Nothing yet. No record exists until the form is handed in.
  2. Step 2 · Who
    You Guardian, if needed

    Complete Section A — and Section B if it applies

    Section A: your name, TRN, mailing address, parish, date of birth, gender, occupation and phone or e-mail.

    Section B is completed by a parent, guardian, field officer or institution when the applicant is under {{ guardian_age }}, incapacitated or in institutional care. Bring the guardian's ID (driver's licence, passport or voter ID).

    Sign the declaration that you live in Jamaica.

    What NHF staff see
    Still nothing — this happens on paper.
  3. Step 3 · Who
    Your doctor

    Your doctor certifies your conditions (Section C)

    The doctor ticks each covered illness you have, marks it mild or severe, writes the total number of conditions, their office address and phone and their Medical Council (MCJ) number, then signs.

    Pap smear or PSA only? If you have no chronic illness and only want these screening benefits, skip Section C — no doctor's signature is needed.

    What NHF staff see
    Later, the MCJ number is checked against the NHF Doctors register. An unknown number is sent to the Medical Council to confirm.
  4. Step 4 · Who
    You Customer Care agent

    Hand the form in at any card centre

    The agent keys the form into the system in the same order as the paper, records the serial number printed on the form, and checks your TRN with the Tax Office. You can sign on the signature pad.

    You get a pick-up slip with your tracking number.

    What NHF staff see
    Tracking no. created · TRN ✓ matched
    Name and date of birth locked to the Tax Office record
    Form serial · Sections A, B, C
    Who can change it: the agent at the centre that received it. Staff at other centres can see the status only.
  5. Step 5 · Who
    The system Agent / supervisor

    Eligibility checks run as it is keyed

    Every save checks the form: guardian for minors, conditions that fit your age and sex, the doctor's MCJ number and signature, your signature, and contact details.

    From age {{ jadep_age }}, JADEP is added automatically.

    What NHF staff see
    ● Must fix e.g. signature missing
    ● Check e.g. condition total differs
    ● Ready to approve
    Who can change it: the agent, or a supervisor it is assigned to. Medical details are shown only to staff who process eligibility.
  6. Step 6 · Who
    Customer Care agent

    Approved — card printed

    You get an NHF member number and your card is printed at the centre. Your benefits start straight away.

    What NHF staff see
    Approved · member 2000000123
    Card v1 queued for printing
    Who can change it: nobody edits an approved form. Later changes use a change request.
  7. Step 7 · Who
    You Registration clerk

    Collect your card

    Show your pick-up slip. The clerk hands over the card and records the collection. Someone else can collect it with a signed letter from you and your TRN card.

    What NHF staff see
    Card collected · date · clerk
{# ===== By mail ===== #}
  1. Step 1 · Who
    You Guardian Doctor

    Complete Sections A, B and C

    Exactly as for in-person: Section A by you, Section B by a guardian when needed, Section C by your doctor (not needed for Pap smear / PSA only). Sign the declaration.

    What NHF staff see
    Nothing yet.
  2. Step 2 · Who
    You

    Post it with a photocopy of your TRN

    Mail the completed form and a photocopy of your TRN to:

    {{ p.mail_address }}

    Allow {{ p.mail_turnaround }}.

    What NHF staff see
    Nothing until the envelope is opened.
  3. Step 3 · Who
    Head Office staff

    Head Office keys the form

    Staff enter it as a mailed application with the date it was received, record the form serial number and confirm the TRN photocopy is enclosed. The TRN is checked with the Tax Office.

    What NHF staff see
    Channel: By mail · received date
    TRN photocopy enclosed ✓
    TRN ✓ matched
    Who can change it: the Head Office team that received it.
  4. Step 4 · Who
    The system Head Office staff

    Checks, and a call if something is missing

    The same eligibility checks as in person. If something is missing or the TRN doesn't match, NHF contacts you using the phone or e-mail on the form.

    What NHF staff see
    Must fix / Check / Ready to approve
    Contact log of every call or letter
  5. Step 5 · Who
    Staff You

    Approved — collect your card

    Your card is printed for the collection centre you chose on the form, and you are told when it is ready.

    What NHF staff see
    Approved · card sent to your collection centre · you are notified
{# ===== Online via doctor ===== #}
  1. Step 1 · Who
    You

    Take your TRN card to your doctor

    Your doctor must be registered for the NHF doctor portal. Give them your TRN and a mobile number where NHF can reach you.

    What NHF staff see
    Nothing yet.
  2. Step 2 · Who
    Your doctor

    The doctor completes the application online

    The doctor enters your name, TRN and mobile (Section A), certifies your illnesses (Section C) with their MCJ number and digital signature, and chooses the centre where you will collect the card.

    What NHF staff see
    Nothing until the doctor submits.
  3. Step 3 · Who
    The system Head Office online unit

    NHF receives a signed electronic form

    The form arrives in the online queue. Your TRN is checked with the Tax Office and the doctor with the Medical Council register automatically.

    What NHF staff see
    Online · portal reference
    TRN ✓ · doctor ✓ confirmed
    Ready to approve
    Who can change it: the Head Office online unit.
  4. Step 4 · Who
    Head Office online unit

    Approved — card printed at your centre

    The card is printed at the centre your doctor chose and you get a message when it is ready.

    What NHF staff see
    Approved · card at collection centre · SMS sent
  5. Step 5 · Who
    You Registration clerk

    Collect your card

    Bring your TRN card. The clerk confirms who you are, hands over the card and records the collection.

    What NHF staff see
    Card collected · date · clerk
{# ---------- Which sections ---------- #}

Which sections do I fill in?

Section A

Always

Applicant information: name, TRN, address, parish, date of birth, gender, occupation, phone or e-mail.

Section B

When someone applies for you

Under {{ guardian_age }}, incapacitated, or in institutional care. Completed by a parent, guardian, field officer or institution, with their ID.

Section C

For a chronic illness

Completed and signed by your doctor with their MCJ number. Not needed if you only want a Pap smear or PSA test.

{# ---------- Already have a card ---------- #}

Already have a card?

Add or update a condition

Tick Update medical record in Section A and have your doctor complete Section C. Your record is updated within {{ p.record_update_hours }} hours. You keep the same card — no new card is printed.

Lost or damaged card

Tick Replace card. Only your name, TRN and signature are needed. Same day at any card centre.

Change your details

Tick Change member details for a new address, phone or collection centre. A name or date of birth change comes from your Tax Office record.

{# ---------- Double subsidies ---------- #}

Double subsidy

Children under {{ child_age }}

Get twice the NHF subsidy on medication and devices. Worked out from the date of birth — nothing extra to bring.

Persons living with a disability

Registered with the JCPD? Bring your JCPD ID card or interim disability certificate letter when you apply, or to any card centre to update your card.
